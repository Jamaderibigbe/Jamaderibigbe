### 👤 About Me

I currently work in Backend Operations in the fintech industry, where a core part of my role involves data analysis. I dig into transaction and operational data, track KPIs, build dashboards, and create reports that help the business understand what is actually happening beneath the surface. That operational vantage point means I do not just analyze data; I understand the systems and processes that generate it in the first place.

That combination of operations exposure and hands on analysis is what is pulling me toward Data Engineering. I want to become the person who builds the reliable pipelines and infrastructure that operational and analytical teams depend on every day. Instead of chasing tutorials, I build production style projects: real pipelines, real APIs, real orchestration, because that is how I actually learn and how I want to be evaluated.
